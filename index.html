How we work

Six steps, one team.

Every order follows the same careful path, so quality stays the same on every piece.

  1. Artwork

    We receive your design or create one, then prepare separations.

  2. Screens

    Screens are coated and exposed in-house.

  3. Colour match

    Shades are mixed and approved against your reference.

  4. Sample

    A strike-off is printed for your approval.

  5. Production

    Bulk printing on M&R or manual machines, then curing.

  6. Delivery

    Checked, counted and delivered within 45 km.
